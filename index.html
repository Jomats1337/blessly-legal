Blessly

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Who we are

Jomats FZCO provides Blessly: Bible Study & Prayer for iPhone and iPad. This policy describes how Blessly and its service providers process information when you use the app and its account services. You can reach us at support@blessly.me.

What we collect and why

  • Account and sign-in. Blessly can create a device-based account before you provide an email address. We use an app-generated device identifier and a Blessly account identifier to recognize your account, connect progress to it, and protect access. If you register or sign in, we receive your email address, account credentials or authentication information, and any name and identity information supplied through Sign in with Apple or Google. Apple may provide a relay email address. We use this information to sign you in, verify or recover access, send account messages, and recognize eligible memberships.
  • Journey and preferences. We process lesson and activity progress, quiz responses in lessons, reflections you save, and your chosen Biblical Archetype if saved to your account. We use them to show your progress, resume your Journey, and personalize relevant features. Your reflections and conversations can reveal religious beliefs or other sensitive information. Bible reader settings, verse highlights and notes, Study Plan progress, and listening preferences are stored on your device.
  • AI Companion. When you send a message, Blessly stores conversation messages, responses, feedback you provide, and related safety information. We send the relevant message and conversation context to OpenAI to generate responses and, where applicable, help moderate requests. Context can include your first name, language, time zone, Journey progress, and a reflection you explicitly choose to share with the Companion. Text you enter may disclose health or other sensitive information. We use safety signals to identify potentially harmful situations and respond with appropriate resources.
  • Scan & Study. When you use the camera or choose an image, text recognition takes place on your device. Blessly sends the selected Bible reference and request information to its service to retrieve or generate a study. We do not upload the page photo or the text recognized from it. Our service can send the corresponding public-domain Bible passage to OpenAI to generate study material.
  • Subscriptions and prior memberships. Apple processes App Store payments. Blessly and Adapty receive purchase and subscription records such as transaction identifiers, product, price, currency, subscription status, and related account identifiers. We use them to manage access and trial eligibility, restore purchases, and understand subscription performance. For existing web memberships, we may use the purchaser email and records from Solidgate to check membership. Payment providers handle payment credentials.
  • Technical data and attribution. When the app connects to a service, that service receives request information such as the IP address. Blessly and its providers process device and app identifiers, device and software details, language, time zone, storefront or approximate location inferred from network information, diagnostic records, and app activity such as launches, paywall views, or purchases. We use this information to deliver the service, investigate errors, protect accounts, understand product use, and measure acquisition campaigns.

Who receives information

  • Apple and Google provide sign-in when selected. Apple also provides App Store billing and subscription controls.
  • OpenAI processes request information as described for AI Companion and Scan & Study.
  • Adapty and AppsFlyer support subscriptions and acquisition measurement. Blessly can send limited campaign information and an AppsFlyer identifier to Adapty. Adapty can send subscription lifecycle events, including trial, renewal, cancellation, and refund information, to AppsFlyer with transaction and attribution identifiers. Campaign and conversion information may be used for campaign measurement through configured advertising integrations. We do not send the text of your Companion messages, reflections, or Bible notes to these services for attribution.
  • Brevo delivers account-related messages, such as sign-in verification and password recovery. Solidgate supports existing web memberships. Our hosting, security, and content delivery providers, including Cloudflare, handle network requests, infrastructure, and content delivery.

When you choose to open an external link or share a verse through another app, the service you select handles the resulting information. We may also disclose information when legally required or to investigate fraud, protect users, or address a security incident. Providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Retention and deletion

We keep account details and server-backed content while your account is active or while needed to provide the relevant feature. You can delete conversations from Companion history. Diagnostic and security records are kept as needed to investigate issues and prevent misuse. Transaction and related records may be kept to verify access, comply with legal and accounting obligations, prevent fraud, or resolve disputes.

To delete a signed-in account, open Journey → Profile → Settings → Delete account. This requests deletion of your account and its associated personal feature data from Blessly’s active systems. The Delete my data control in Settings clears local app data. If you no longer have the app or need help identifying a device-based account, email support@blessly.me. We may ask for enough information to verify your request. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment card details.

Backup copies, diagnostic logs, subscription provider records, and legacy payment records may be retained separately for recovery, billing, security, or legal purposes. Deleting your Blessly account does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel an App Store subscription from Apple Subscriptions.

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, to restrict or object to its use, or to withdraw consent when processing depends on consent. Email support@blessly.me to make a request or ask about your options. You can change camera permissions in your device settings and use a typed reference for Scan & Study.

If you believe a child has provided information without permission required by applicable law, contact us so we can investigate. We may update this policy and will revise the effective date when we do.
